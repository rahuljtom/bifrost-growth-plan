The AI Model Exit Plan

    What I need, and what could go wrong

    A small team and five decisions.

    • Engineering: 2–3 days for exit-test, then about 20% of an engineer for reviews (30 × 45 min ≈ 23 hours).
    • Founder: about 2 hours a week for bylines, Sarvam and the partner call.
    • Sales: a US salesperson who picks up leads within 24 hours.
    • Decisions: OK to cite regulators and name competitors; a partner margin a ~20%; the ACV to model pipeline; whether Edge or the core gateway is the Enterprise priority.
    • The June news fades: I lead with retirements and price changes, which never stop.
    • Validation fails: the week-1 kill rule switches the spine.
    • It reads as political: I frame it as continuity, not sovereignty.
